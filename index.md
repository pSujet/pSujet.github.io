Hi, I'm Sujet Phodapol — a control and mechanical engineer from Thailand, currently working as a Robotics Control Engineer at <a href="https://www.kth.se/en" style="text-decoration: none; color: black">Nio Robotics</a> in Toulouse, France. 

<br><br>

My passion lies at the intersection of control theory and mechanical design, where I strive to create innovative solutions for complex robotic systems — especially in areas like <strong>optimization-based control</strong>, <strong>space and legged robotics</strong>, and <strong>complex mechanical and actuator design</strong>. To learn more about my research and ongoing work, feel free to visit my <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/publications/" style="text-decoration: none; color: black">publications</a> and <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/projects/" style="text-decoration: none; color: black">projects</a>, or see my <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/about/cv/CV_Sujet_Phodapol.pdf" style="text-decoration: none; color: black; font-weight: bold;" target="_blank">CV</a>. 

<br><br>
I'm always eager to connect with like-minded people and explore new opportunities in the field of robotics. Whether you're curious about my work, interested in collaboration, or just want to chat about robots — feel free to reach out at sujet [dot] ph [at] gmail [dot] com.
